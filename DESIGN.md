# Canopy visual system

## Direction

Canopy behaves like a nocturnal phenology station: each market is a measured plot, its immutable oracle rule is a physical field label, and block progress advances along a seasonal observation rail. The system refuses the familiar trading terminal and generic crypto dashboard. It uses cultivated rows, specimen labels, observation ticks, and clipped measurement windows as functional structure.

The browser is used at a desk during focused technical work under low ambient light, so the ground is true black. The composition is restrained and data-led, with large dark fields and sparse, bright measurements.

## Palette

- `--soil: #000000` — page ground.
- `--bed: #111827` — raised work surfaces.
- `--furrow: #1f2937` — controls and selected plot rows.
- `--rule: #374151` — measured divisions and inactive stems.
- `--copy: #d1d5db` and `--muted: #9ca3af` — readable text.
- `--verified: #19d184` — connected, confirmed, settled, and primary actions only.
- `--datum: #bfff00` — amounts, block heights, ratios, and interactive measurements.
- `--oracle: #ff1dce` — HTTP/JQ machine-executed evidence only.
- `--pending: #facc15` — scheduled, closing, retries, and execution fees.
- `--failed: #ef4444` — invalid, failed, and destructive states.

Green is never botanical decoration. Plant-like geometry stays gray until a state is genuinely verified.

## Typography

- Display: Archivo, heavy and tightly composed, for market questions and the Canopy wordmark.
- UI/body: Barlow Semi Condensed for compact but human operational copy.
- Data: Fira Code for addresses, block numbers, targets, pool amounts, and oracle rules.
- Headings use sentence case. Labels are concise and uppercase only where they behave like specimen tags.

## Composition

The desktop shell has a narrow specimen index, a wide live plot, and a fixed observation rail. Market rows vary in height according to lifecycle importance; equal card grids are not used. The primary market is a full-width cultivation bed with the YES and NO pools growing horizontally from a shared origin.

On compact screens, the index becomes a horizontal plot selector and the observation rail becomes stepped checkpoints. The market question and action always precede supporting mechanism detail.

## Components and states

- Plot labels have square corners, clipped notches, and one punched status hole.
- Market surfaces use a single border or an offset shadow, never both.
- Buttons are transparent, bordered, and state their transaction action. Primary actions use verified green.
- Pending work is shown with gold moving observation ticks; failures use red plus a textual recovery action.
- HTTP and JQ evidence uses magenta braces and a literal oracle-rule readout.
- Focus is a two-pixel green ring with black offset. Disabled controls remain legible and explain why.

## Motion

One authored motion carries the system: a block-height observation sweep advances across the active plot and reveals the next lifecycle checkpoint. It stops under reduced motion. Other transitions are short changes of clip, shadow, and position; content is visible before animation.

## Imagery

Core UI, plots, labels, rails, and status marks are semantic HTML/CSS/SVG. No stock photography, decorative gradients, glass, noise overlays, or generic crypto illustrations. Synthetic sample market values are always labeled `LOCAL SAMPLE`.

## Accessibility

Text and controls meet WCAG AA contrast. Color always pairs with shape or text. Keyboard focus is obvious, controls have minimum 44px targets, motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, and market state is exposed in plain language.
